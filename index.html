◇Contract intelligence / production work

SpendRule

When an agreement changes, an invoice decision must change with it.

Problem

Healthcare pricing authority can be scattered across scans, amendments, rate tables, facilities, services, and effective dates. A plausible answer can still point to the wrong governing term.

Hard constraint

A financial conclusion needs the right source, scope, and time. When those cannot be established, the system must surface the ambiguity for review.

Architectural move

Compile versioned contract evidence, use models for semantic matching, and keep pricing and policy checks deterministic. Preserve the path from source passage to line-level decision.

Evidence / artifact

A production validation flow with source-linked decisions, versioned contract records, and a review outcome for unresolved cases.

What this reveals

I treat provenance and uncertainty as product behavior, not hidden implementation details.

▣On-device safety / team project

SafeScreen AI

A visual safety layer that does not need to see your screen from a server.

Problem

Safety tools often ask people to upload the very screen content they want protected. An intervention also has to arrive while the content is still on screen.

Hard constraint

Screen frames stay on the phone. The Android app requests no internet permission, and a weak manipulation signal cannot become a definitive warning.

Architectural move

Run detection on-device, pass its output through an explicit policy layer, and blur across apps. Keep deepfake detection as a qualified secondary badge because its out-of-distribution evaluation was near chance.

Evidence / artifact

Working Android screen monitor and blur overlay, validated on device; first place at the Qualcomm × Meta ExecuTorch Hackathon. Winners recap · Team entry

What this reveals

I prefer promises a user can verify in permissions and behavior, and I make weak model signals visibly uncertain.

⌁Agent systems / team project

ARIA

An agent may suggest a trade. It should not grant itself permission to make one.

Problem

Autonomous trading agents can produce convincing rationales without making safe decisions. A trace is only useful if it also records what the system blocked.

Hard constraint

The agent operates in paper trading. Position, exposure, and rate limits are enforced in code rather than requested in a prompt.

Architectural move

Separate strategy reasoning from a risk router with code-enforced gates. Sign decision checkpoints and retain rationale and rejection records for later inspection.

Evidence / artifact

A paper-trading system with signed checkpoints, ERC-8004 attestations, and rejection logs; second place in a hackathon prize track. Team entry · Source code

What this reveals

I separate what a model recommends from what the product permits, then preserve the evidence for both outcomes.

From the field notebook

The evaluation unit changes the product.

Chronoscope is an active exploration in visual search across historical encyclopedia illustrations. Its key design decision is to judge the exact image crop or text chunk returned to a person, not a whole page that happens to contain something relevant.

Human labels remain ground truth; model judgments are review aids. That distinction is small in the interface and fundamental to whether a search-quality claim means anything.

Operating principles

My default is to make the hard question visible.

Where did the answer come from?

Source identity, effective dates, and the exact cited artifact should travel with a conclusion. That is how a reviewer can challenge it.

SpendRule / Chronoscope

What is the model allowed to do?

Interpretation is useful. Financial rules, trade limits, and safety interventions need explicit policies that can be inspected and tested.

SpendRule / ARIA / SafeScreen

What happens when it is wrong?

Give uncertainty a real destination: human review, a qualified badge, or a blocked action with a recorded reason.
