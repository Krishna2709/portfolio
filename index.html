◇Contract intelligence / production work

SpendRule

The answer has to survive the journey from inbound file to client decision.

Problem

Healthcare invoices and governing agreements arrive through different systems, often as scans or archives. Pricing authority may be split across amendments, rate tables, facilities, and effective dates.

Hard constraint

A financial conclusion needs the right source, scope, and time. An accepted upload event is not proof a document was processed; retries and replay must preserve identity and avoid duplicate decisions.

Architectural move

Route SFTP and dropzone files through event-driven archive and document handlers into a versioned vault. Run extraction, contract compilation, and invoice validation as durable stages. Use models for semantic matching, deterministic checks for pricing, and traces and audit paths to examine the result.

Evidence / artifact

A production validation flow with content-addressed document storage, event-driven intake and recovery, source-linked decisions, and an asynchronous audit of validation outcomes.

What this reveals

I design the operating and recovery path alongside the AI decision path. Provenance matters from the first file transfer to the final explanation.

≋Legal AI / platform engineering

Casey / 108-AI

Legal AI needs a workflow a reviewer can follow, not just an answer.

Problem

Contract review, clause drafting, comparison, and multi-document Q&A draw on different parts of a legal corpus. The work spans document processing, retrieval, model judgment, and a lawyer's review surface.

Hard constraint

Requests must stay within the right tenant and document context. Long-running review jobs need explicit state, retry and failure behavior, and a trace that shows how a recommendation was formed.

Architectural move

Separate ingestion and hybrid retrieval from LangGraph review, drafting, and validation stages. Wrap longer jobs in event-driven execution with status and failure handling; instrument graph nodes and model calls for inspection.

Evidence / artifact

Implemented contract-scoring and playbook-customization graphs, event-triggered workflows with failure handling, and document Q&A paths with grounding and citation checks.

What this reveals

I shape agent capabilities as bounded services with observable state, so product teams and reviewers can inspect the path to an output.

▣On-device safety / team project

SafeScreen AI

A visual safety layer that does not need to see your screen from a server.

Problem

Safety tools often ask people to upload the very screen content they want protected. An intervention also has to arrive while the content is still on screen.

Hard constraint

Screen frames stay on the phone. The Android app requests no internet permission, and a weak manipulation signal cannot become a definitive warning.

Architectural move

Run detection on-device, pass its output through an explicit policy layer, and blur across apps. Keep deepfake detection as a qualified secondary badge because its out-of-distribution evaluation was near chance.

Evidence / artifact

Working Android screen monitor and blur overlay, validated on device; first place at the Qualcomm × Meta ExecuTorch Hackathon. Winners recap · Team entry

What this reveals

I prefer promises a user can verify in permissions and behavior, and I make weak model signals visibly uncertain.

⌁Agent systems / team project

ARIA

An agent may suggest a trade. It should not grant itself permission to make one.

Problem

Autonomous trading agents can produce convincing rationales without making safe decisions. A trace is only useful if it also records what the system blocked.

Hard constraint

The agent operates in paper trading. Position, exposure, and rate limits are enforced in code rather than requested in a prompt.

Architectural move

Separate strategy reasoning from a risk router with code-enforced gates. Sign decision checkpoints and retain rationale and rejection records for later inspection.

Evidence / artifact

A paper-trading system with signed checkpoints, ERC-8004 attestations, and rejection logs; second place in a hackathon prize track. Team entry · Source code

What this reveals

I separate what a model recommends from what the product permits, then preserve the evidence for both outcomes.

From the field notebook

The evaluation unit changes the product.

Chronoscope is an active exploration in visual search across historical encyclopedia illustrations. Its key design decision is to judge the exact image crop or text chunk returned to a person, not a whole page that happens to contain something relevant.

Human labels remain ground truth; model judgments are review aids. That distinction is small in the interface and fundamental to whether a search-quality claim means anything.
